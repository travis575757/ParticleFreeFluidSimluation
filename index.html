Mass-Conserving Eulerian Liquid Simulation

A browser implementation of Chentanez & Müller (SCA 2012). The whole solver runs on the GPU as WebGL2 shader passes: conservative density advection, density sharpening with local mass conservation, and a multigrid pressure solve with separating solid boundaries.

2D simulator13 scenes, mouse push / pour / drain, moving obstacles, floating rigid bodies, field views. 3D simulator9 scenes, ray-marched liquid surface and density volume, orbit camera, the same interactions in 3D.

What you are looking at

The liquid is a density field ρ on a regular staggered grid; the surface is its 0.5 iso-contour. The paper's contribution is that this field is transported so that the total mass never changes, without particles:

PaperStepHow it runs here
Sec. 3.3Velocity extrapolationPull–push over the grid hierarchy
Sec. 3.4Conservative density advectionBackward gather, then three scatter passes drawn as GPU points with additive float blending
Sec. 3.5Sharpening with local mass conservationRemoved density is traced up the density gradient and re-deposited (Algorithm 2)
Sec. 3.6Solids cutting cells, moving solidsAnalytic distance fields → area and volume fractions; excess density is pushed out of solids
Sec. 3.7IncompressibilityVariational discretisation, ghost-fluid free surface, projected red–black Gauss–Seidel multigrid
Sec. 3.8Sub-grid detailOptional density post-process before the surface is extracted

Total mass drifts by only a few parts per million over hundreds of steps in every scene (32-bit float round-off), even at time steps where the liquid crosses more than ten cells per step. With plain semi-Lagrangian advection the same dam break loses 90 % of its mass in ten seconds; you can switch between the two under Solver.

Running it

  • Serve this folder over HTTP (ES modules do not load from file://): node tools/serve.mjs or python3 -m http.server 8080, then open http://localhost:8080/.
  • Needs WebGL2 with EXT_color_buffer_float, EXT_float_blend and OES_texture_float_linear (any recent desktop browser on a discrete or integrated GPU).
  • README.md lists every place where the paper leaves a choice open and what was chosen, and the one extension that is on by default.
